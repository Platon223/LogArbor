Errors

If you don't see your logs coming in that likely means that your log function was set up incorrectly.

If the log function fails, you will recieve an email explaining what you did wrong.

Don't get the email and don't see your logs?

If you don't see your logs and don't see an email, there's two options what went wrong.

1. Rate limit exceeded(meaning your app or script is sending more than 600 logs per minute). LogArbor is built for startups or small companies and it is not built for enterprise level companies having thousands logs per minute. 2. Your service has 600 logs already. In the next version of LogArbor we will have different packages you can buy and have a bigger log volume allowed.
