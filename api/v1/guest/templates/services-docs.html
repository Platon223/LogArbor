Services

A service represents an application, microservice, or environment. The service_id in your log function points the log to the specific service.

Each service has independent logs and retention rules. When a service is created a retention date is set. What that means is that when a log comes to a service after its retention date all the logs that are in that service are gonna be deleted and a new retention date is going to be set. The retention in a service is fixed and cannot be changed. The retention is 7 days after its creation. For example, if you create a service right now, then all the logs are going to be deleted for that service in 7 days.

This keeps your services clean by deleting the logs that were long ago.

How to create a Service?

To create a service go to Services page and click "+ New Service".

Then fill out the name and the alert level fields.

Once you created your service, you can check different metrics for that service in the Dashboard page. Also, you can check Logs page for incoming logs in that service.
