Troubleshooting

v1.0.0

Don't see your logs?

If you go to Logs page and don't see your logs, this is probably because your log function failed. Check your inbox not the Alerts page because log function fails are not meant to mix with bad logs incoming in your service. So that email will explain why the log function failed.

Don't get the "Why log function failed email"?

If you don't get the email and you are not seeing the logs, there are 2 options of what might be happening:

1. Rate limit exceeded(meaning your app or script is sending more than 600 logs per minute). LogArbor is built for startups or small companies and it is not built for enterprise level companies having thousands logs per minute. 2. Your service has 600 logs already. In the next version of LogArbor we will have different packages you can buy and have a bigger log volume allowed.

You tried everything and still don't see your logs?

If nothing works or makes sense, please contact our support: LogArbor Support Team
